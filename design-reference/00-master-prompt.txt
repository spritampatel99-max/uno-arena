--------------------------------------------------------
G. GAMEPLAY RULES
--------------------------------------------------------

Implement standard UNO-inspired turn-based gameplay.

CARD MATCHING:

A card can be played when:

- Its colour matches the active colour.
- Its number matches the top discard card.
- Its symbol matches the top discard card.
- It is a Wild card.
- It is a Wild Draw Four card when legal.

NUMBER CARDS:

Numbers 0–9.

Effect:
No special action.

SKIP:

The next player loses their turn.

REVERSE:

Changes the direction of play.

DRAW TWO:

The next player draws two cards and loses their turn
under the standard rules.

WILD:

The player selects the next active colour.

WILD DRAW FOUR:

The player selects the next active colour.
The next player draws four cards and loses their turn.

Apply the standard legality restriction for Wild Draw Four.

DRAWING:

When a player cannot play:

- Allow drawing according to the selected rules.
- Apply the appropriate turn rules.
- Do not allow invalid plays.

IMPORTANT:

Keep the rule implementation consistent.

Do not mix house rules with official rules without
clearly identifying them.
